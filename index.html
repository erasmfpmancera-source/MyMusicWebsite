About Me

Hi, I'm Eras Maurel Franquo P. Mancera, but you can call me Eras or Jameer. I'm a 20-year-old music producer from the Philippines.

I've been producing music since I was 10 years old. I started out making beats in FL Studio, and recently I've been recording my voice on BandLab to turn those beats into full tracks. Music is my passion, and I'm constantly experimenting with new sounds and styles.

When I'm not producing, I'm usually listening to music, exploring new genres, or working on my next project. Thanks for visiting my website and checking out my work!
